Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. What data we collect

We only collect financial entry data that you voluntarily input, including transaction titles and amounts.

2. How we use your data

Your data is strictly used to calculate your budget, income, and expenses within the application. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Cookie choices & Storage

This application uses localStorage (a form of essential browser storage) to save your budget entries and Cookie Banner preferences directly on your device. No data is sent to external servers.
